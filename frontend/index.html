End-to-End System Architecture

Production Capstone Design

1. Multilingual Corpus

2,200 synthetic student evaluations across 7 courses, 6 semesters, and 3 languages (EN, HI, Hinglish).

2. Trilingual Preprocessor

Negation preservation ("nahi", "mat", "not"), Unicode Devanagari normalization, and phoneme mapping.

3. 12-Aspect ABSA Engine

Comprehensive rule & semantic lexicon mapping capturing Course Content, Labs, Grading, Instructor, etc.

4. BERTopic Temporal Drift

Classifies topics into Emerging, Declining, Persistent, and New based on linear regression slopes across semesters.
